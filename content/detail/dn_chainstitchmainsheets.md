<div class="h1big">Chain-Stitch Mainsheets for the DN Iceboat:</div>
	<div class="h2">Note: color may vary.</div>
	<hr />
	<p>
		The chain-stitch (aka "braided") mainsheet has been used in the DN class for many years.<br />
		Originally it was constructed with 3/16" line, but the knots have trouble going through the 40mm blocks
		that many sailors use between the boom and deck at the back of the DN.<br />
		Many people have made sheets from 4mm line to reduce the size of the knots.
	</p><p>
		We now offer three different mainsheet designs constructed from 3/16", 4mm, and a composite of 3mm polyester
		with a load-bearing 3mm Dyneema "core".
	</p>
	<div class="img650">
		<a href="/products/IMG_6806_950.jpg target="_blank"><span class="img650"><img src="/products/IMG_6806_650.jpg width="650" height="433" /></span></a>
	</div>
	<p>
		Here is a comparison of the size of the three sheets.
	</p><p>
		The 3/16" line (left) has about 3/4" diameter, the 4mm line (center) is about 5/8", and the composite 3mm (right) is about 1/2".
	</p><p>
		The 3/16" line will go through 57mm blocks, but takes a tug to start it through a 40mm block.<br />
		I haven't sailed with the 4mm mainsheet yet, but I would guess is goes through 40mm blocks much more eaisly than the 3/16".<br />
		The 3mm coomposite goes through 40mm blocks very easily, and will run through a 29mm block with a little bit of help (I use the sheet on my blokart<sup class="reg">®</sup> landsailer).
	</p>
