<div class="h1">Prototype Chainstitch Mainsheet:</div>
	<div class="h2">Last Update: June 10, 2014 - First photos of protoype #1</div>
	<hr />
	<div class="h3">
		5/28/2014 - Here are two photos of the first protoype "composite" DN chainstitch mainsheet.
	</div>
	<p class="img650">
		 This version is made from 3mm hot-pink double-braid polyester line chain-stitched around a blue 3mm Dyneema<sup class="reg">®</sup> single-braid "core".
	</p>
	<p class="img650">
		 The intent is to make a mainsheet with a slightly smaller diameter in the chain-stitch portion while still maintaining the good
		 grip provided by the knots.
	</p>
	<p class="img650">
		I was very surprised that after I had tied the first half-dozen knots the central line was securely bound to the double-braid. This meant that sewing the two together
		wasn't necessary. The whipping is just there to protect the spot where the double-braid enters the Dyneema<sup class="reg">®</sup> to make a smooth transition. If you look closely at the lower photo
		you can see that the diameter of the blue line gets smaller as the double-braid inside is tapered away.
	</p>
	<p class="img650">
		I've included a section of the more typical mainsheet constructed from 3/16" double-braid for a size comparison.
	</p>
