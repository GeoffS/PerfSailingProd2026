<div class="h1big">3/16" Sta-Set Chain-Stitch Mainsheet:</div>
	<div class="h2">Note: color may vary.<br />
	(you can special order a sheet in any color of 3/16" Sta-Set line)</div>
	<hr />
	<p class="img650">
		The sheet is symetric, to you can end-for-end it when one tail wears out.<br />
		The tails are 108" long so the end of the tail will be at the front of the boom when sheeted in all the way.<br />
		The center section is about 9' long so you can let the sheet out as far as needed during normal sailing and still have
		the chain-stiched/braided section in your hands.
	</p>
