<div class="h1big">Tool-Free DN Hound Attachment Hardware:</div>
	<div class="h2">Note: the hound fitting is not included.</div>
	<hr />
	<p class="img650">
		This kit contians the parts you need to securely attach a <i>Strubel hound</i> to your mast without tools.
	</p>
	<p class="img650">
		It contains a 1" long 1/4-28 aircraft bolt (AN4-10), castle-nut, and safety pin.
	</p>
	<p class="img650">
		Aircraft hardware is used because the un-threaded part of the bolt is only 1/16" shorter than
		the hound fitting width. This gives the most strength possible to this critical connection.<br />
		The threads on aircraft hardware are also more precisely cut than ordinary bolts, so the nut
		threads on very smoothly.
	</p>
