<p class="normal"><span class="inlineCompanyName">Performance Sailing Products</span> specializes in custom fabrication and 
small production runs of hardware for high-performance wind sports. 
</p>
<p class="normal">
We have a number of "standard" products that we sell and we are always interested in helping with one-off custom projects. <a href="Contact.html">Give us a call!</a>
</p>
